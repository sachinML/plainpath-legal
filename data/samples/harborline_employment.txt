EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

This Employment Agreement is made on January 15, 2026 between Harborline Analytics,
Inc. (the "Employer") and Sam Rivera (the "Employee").

1. Position. Employee will work as a Data Specialist. Employment is at-will. Either
party may terminate employment at any time, with or without cause, by giving fourteen
(14) days written notice when practical.

2. Compensation. Employee shall be paid a salary of $72,000.00 per year, paid
biweekly by direct deposit.

3. Duties. Employee shall perform the duties in the job description and other tasks
reasonably assigned.

4. Intellectual property. Employee hereby assigns to Employer all inventions, works,
and intellectual property created during employment that relate to Employer's business.
Such work is work made for hire.

5. Non-compete. For eighteen (18) months after employment ends, Employee shall not
work for a competing analytics company within fifty (50) miles of Employer's office
and shall not solicit Employer's customers.

6. Arbitration. Employee agrees that covered disputes will be resolved by binding
arbitration and waives any right to participate in a class or collective action.
Employee also waives the right to a jury trial.

7. Confidentiality. Employee shall not disclose confidential information during
employment and for three (3) years after.

8. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

SAMPLE ONLY. This is a fictional teaching document. It is not a real contract and is
not legal advice. Non-compete rules vary widely; this sample is intentionally dense.
