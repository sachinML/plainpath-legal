RESIDENTIAL LEASE RENEWAL (FICTIONAL SAMPLE — COMPARE WITH SAMPLE A)

This Renewal Lease is entered into as of February 1, 2027 by and between
Oakridge Court LLC (the "Landlord") and Jordan Hale (the "Tenant") for the dwelling
located at 414 Willow Lane, Unit 2B, Cedar Falls.

1. Term. The renewal term begins on March 1, 2027 and ends on February 29, 2028.
This lease shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless Tenant
gives fifteen (15) days written notice prior to the anniversary date.

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,595.00 per month, due on the first calendar day
of each month. A late fee of $160.00 may be charged if rent is more than three (3)
days past due.

3. Security deposit. The existing security deposit of $1,450.00 will continue to be
held. This renewal does not state when the deposit shall be returned.

4. Entry. Landlord may enter the premises at any time to inspect, show, or make
repairs. No advance notice is required.

5. Early termination. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay
an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

6. Unilateral changes. Landlord may modify house rules at any time without notice and
in its sole discretion.

7. Disputes. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration. Tenant waives any
right to participate in a class or collective action.

8. Governing law. This lease is governed by the laws of the State of Iowa.
