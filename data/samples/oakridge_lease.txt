RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between
Oakridge Court LLC (the "Landlord") and Jordan Hale (the "Tenant") for the dwelling
located at 414 Willow Lane, Unit 2B, Cedar Falls.

1. Term. The lease term begins on March 1, 2026 and ends on February 28, 2027. This
lease does not automatically renew. Either party must give thirty (30) days written
notice if they do not wish to discuss a new agreement.

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,450.00 per month, due on the first calendar day
of each month. A late fee of $45.00 may be charged if rent is more than five (5) days
past due.

3. Security deposit. Tenant shall deposit $1,450.00 as a security deposit. Landlord
shall return the security deposit within twenty-one (21) days after Tenant vacates,
minus documented repair costs beyond ordinary wear.

4. Entry. Landlord may enter the premises to inspect or make repairs after giving
twenty-four (24) hours notice, except in a genuine emergency.

5. Repairs. Landlord shall keep the dwelling habitable and shall make necessary
repairs within a reasonable time after written notice from Tenant.

6. Early termination. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay
an early termination fee of $725.00 in addition to rent through the date the unit is
re-rented.

7. Pets. No pets without written consent. A pet deposit, if approved, will be stated
in an addendum.

8. Notices. Written notice must be delivered to Landlord at 10 Market Street,
Cedar Falls, or to Tenant at the premises.

9. Governing law. This lease is governed by the laws of the State of Iowa.

10. Entire agreement. This document is the entire agreement of the parties.

not legal advice.
